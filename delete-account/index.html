Delete your account

GymDesk · com.rslabs.gymdesk

This page explains how a gym owner can delete their GymDesk account and everything stored with it. It applies to both the Android app and the web app.

Option 1 — in the app (immediate)

  1. Open GymDesk and sign in with your mobile number.
  2. Go to Home → Settings (gear icon), scroll to Account.
  3. Tap Delete account, type DELETE and confirm.

Tip: export your data first from More → Export data if you want a copy.

Option 2 — by email

Email riyazshaikh16in@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my GymDesk account” and the mobile number you sign in with, in international format (for example +919876543210). We may reply once to confirm the request came from you; nothing is deleted until we are sure.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Gym profile, logo, numbering and receipt settingsDeleted
All members, their photos and detailsDeleted
Plans, services, membership recordsDeleted
Payments and receiptsDeleted
GymDesk subscriptionAuto-renew cancelled; billing history deleted
Your login (mobile number)Deleted from Firebase Authentication
One-way hash of your mobile number + free-trial end dateKept, only to prevent a repeat free trial. It cannot be turned back into your number.

Payment records that Razorpay is legally required to keep for GymDesk subscription payments are retained by Razorpay under its own policy.

How long it takes

In-app deletion happens immediately. Email requests are actioned within 30 days, usually much sooner, and we reply to confirm when done.

Deletion is permanent. A deleted account cannot be restored. Signing up again with the same number creates a new, empty gym without a new free trial.

Members of a gym

If you are a gym member and want your details removed, ask your gym. If you cannot reach them, email us with the gym's name and your mobile number and we will pass the request on.
