GymDesk

Privacy Policy

GymDesk by RS Labs · Last updated 26 September 2026

GymDesk helps a gym owner run the front desk: register members, sell plans, record payments, share receipts and send renewal reminders. This page says exactly what we store, why, who else handles it, and how to get it deleted. It covers the Android app (com.rslabs.gymdesk) and the web app at gymdesk-rslabs.web.app.

Who is responsible for what

GymDesk has one kind of user: the gym owner. The owner decides which member details to enter and is responsible for having their members' permission to do so. RS Labs stores and processes that information only on the owner's behalf, to provide the app.

What we collect about the gym owner

  • Mobile number — used only to sign you in with a one-time code (OTP). The SMS is sent by Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Gym profile — gym name, address, phone, email (optional) and logo (optional). These appear on receipts and registration forms.
  • Subscription status — your free-trial dates, the GymDesk plan you chose, and the Razorpay reference for each GymDesk subscription payment.

What the owner stores about members

  • Member details — name, mobile number, and optionally age, gender, address, an emergency contact, notes and a photo.
  • Membership records — plans sold, dates, fees, discounts, payments received (amount, cash / UPI / card, an optional reference such as a UPI transaction ID or the last 4 digits of a card) and receipt numbers.

GymDesk does not collect Aadhaar numbers, and never stores full card numbers, UPI PINs or bank details.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no ad identifiers, and no selling or renting of data.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not message your members. Reminders and receipts are sent by the owner from their own WhatsApp or share sheet; GymDesk only prepares the message or PDF.

Where it is stored and who can see it

  • Records are stored in Google Cloud Firestore in Mumbai, India. Member photos and the gym logo are stored in Google Cloud Storage in the United States. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Google.
  • Each gym's records can be read only by that gym's signed-in owner account; this is enforced by server-side security rules.
  • RS Labs staff access data only when needed to fix a problem you report or to act on a deletion request.

Service providers

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Storage, Cloud Functions, Hosting) — to run the app. Firebase privacy.
  • Razorpay — only if the owner buys a GymDesk subscription. Razorpay collects the payment details on its own page; we receive only the payment status and reference. Razorpay privacy.

How long we keep it

  • Everything is kept while the account exists, so the gym can see its full history.
  • When the owner deletes the account, all gym data, member records and photos are deleted immediately.
  • We keep a one-way hash of the owner's phone number and the free-trial end date after deletion, only to stop the free trial being claimed again. It cannot be turned back into the phone number.
  • Payment records that Razorpay must keep by law are retained by Razorpay under its own policy.

Deleting data

  • Owners: in the app, open Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes the gym, all members, plans, payments, receipts and photos, cancels any GymDesk subscription, and removes the login. See how to delete your account for the other options.
  • Members: ask your gym to remove your details. If you cannot reach them, email us (below) with the gym's name and your mobile number and we will pass the request to the gym owner.
  • Owners can export all their data at any time from More → Export data.

Children

GymDesk is a business tool for gym owners and is not directed at children. A gym may record members under 18; the gym owner is responsible for having a parent or guardian's consent.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect, we will update the date at the top of this page and mention it in the app's release notes.
